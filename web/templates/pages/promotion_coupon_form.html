Coupon

Empty means every code is generated.
Percent for a percentage, an amount for a fixed discount.
Empty for no cap.

Validity

absolute is a fixed window. relative counts from the moment each coupon is issued, which is what "thirty days from now" means.

Issuance

0 for unlimited.

Stacking

The defaults are the cautious ones. A coupon that stacks with nothing and with no member discount cannot be combined by accident into a larger discount than anybody intended.
